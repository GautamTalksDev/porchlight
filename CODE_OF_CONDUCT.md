# Code of Conduct

Porchlight exists to help neighbours look after each other. We expect the same of everyone who works on it.

## What we expect

* Be kind and assume good intent.
* Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
* Give feedback on the work, never on the person.
* Respect that people contribute in different languages and from different backgrounds.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
* Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
* Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainers privately through the email on the repository owner's profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.
